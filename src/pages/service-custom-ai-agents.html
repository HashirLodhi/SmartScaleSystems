Our Process

Building Your
AI Agent

Our agent development process ensures the system integrates seamlessly with your tools and delivers measurable business value from day one.

1. Use Case Definition

Define the agent's task, success criteria, tools it can access, and guardrails it must respect.

2. Tool Integration

Connect the agent to your APIs, databases, and business tools with proper authentication and rate limiting.

3. Agent Development

Build the agent's reasoning logic, tool-use patterns, and response generation with comprehensive error handling.

4. Evaluation and Testing

Test accuracy, tool use, safety, latency, and cost across realistic scenarios before deployment.

5. Deploy and Monitor

Launch with logging, performance dashboards, and alerting to track accuracy and catch issues early.
